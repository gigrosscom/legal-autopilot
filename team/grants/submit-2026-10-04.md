## 2. Обновлённые общие ответы (вставлять вместо старых)

**Mission:** Free a billion people from legal helplessness.

**One-liner (≤150):** AI that reads the law, reasons through your case and shows the path to a solution — then drafts and sends the documents.

**Short description (~450):**
> Konsilier AI is an AI assistant for legal questions. A person describes the situation by voice or text and attaches
> evidence. The AI analyses the facts against official law, cites the source text, picks the route (whom to address,
> where, by when), drafts the claim or complaint, the person e-signs it, and the service sends it and tracks deadlines.
> Free to understand, paid to act. Live in Kazakhstan since 02.10.2026; next markets — the EU and the US.

**Tech:** Conversational + analytical + reasoning AI. Scenario engine (rules choose document, addressee and deadlines),
retrieval of official legal texts before citing, de-identification of personal data. Own legal model in development —
**Konsilier LM**; quality measured on our benchmark **Konsilier Bench**. Documents: Anthropic Claude; chat: Google Gemini.

**Positioning (в каждой анкете):** Konsilier AI is a software product, not a law firm; it does not promise outcomes.

Остальные поля (юрлицо, БИН, адрес, команда, $0 привлечено, pre-revenue) — без изменений, `submit-2026-10-02.md` Б.0.

- Product name: **Konsilier LM**
- Description: *Konsilier LM is our legal language model in development for Russian and Kazakh (English next): retrieval
  over open legal corpora and fine-tuning of open models, evaluated on our own benchmark, Konsilier Bench. It powers
