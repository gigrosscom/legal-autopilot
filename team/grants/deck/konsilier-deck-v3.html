06 · TechnologyKonsilier AI

Grounded AI, not a chatbot

Conversational

Voice-first interview that collects facts in plain language.

Analytical

Rule-based scenario engine picks document, addressee and deadlines — not left to the model.

Deep reasoning

Every statute cited only after the official text is retrieved.

Private

Personal data is de-identified before text reaches a model.

  • ZANN — our own legal language model in development: retrieval over open legal corpora plus fine-tuning of open models, measured on our benchmark Zann-Bench. It replaces third-party models only where it scores better.
  • Country packs (law, scenarios, language) make each new market a data project, not a rebuild.
