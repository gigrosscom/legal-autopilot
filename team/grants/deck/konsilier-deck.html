03 · Product · live at konsilier.comKonsiliér AI

What works today

Services: Kazakhstan coverage by area of law
Web app: start a question in the AI chat
Phone app (PWA) with deadline notifications
  • Free AI chat with a daily message limit, on free Google Gemini models. Documents are drafted by Anthropic Claude.
  • 20 Kazakhstan scenarios, each carried through to a finished document: refunds, unpaid wages, credit fraud, fine appeals, child support and more.
  • Filing guidance and deadline reminders for each scenario. The user is asked to check the details before filing.
  • Telegram bot that takes a case step by step in Russian and Kazakh.
  • Upload instead of typing: the service reads PDFs, photos and DOCX files and asks only for what is missing.
  • Interface in five languages: Russian, Kazakh, English, Turkish and Arabic.
